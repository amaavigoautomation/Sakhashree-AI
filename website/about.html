Sakhashree Neeta

A life Devoted to Healing, Wisdom & Humanity
Spiritual healer Sakhashree Neeta is a globally recognised authority in Chakra Healing and the creator of Rudraksha Ratna Chakra Therapy (RRCT®️), a unique healing system that uses Rudraksha beads and gemstones to balance and harmonise the chakras. For over two decades, she has dedicated her life to helping people overcome emotional pain, stress, anxiety, limiting beliefs, and life's challenges by realigning their thoughts, beliefs, and energy patterns, guiding them towards holistic health, inner strength, and spiritual awakening.

People from all walks of life including CEOs, entrepreneurs, professionals, homemakers, students, and spiritual seekers from across India and around the world seek her guidance. Over the years, more than 20,000 testimonials have reflected the profound transformation experienced by those she has helped. Known for her compassionate approach and ability to listen without judgement, she is lovingly regarded by many as a mentor, and trusted friend, creating a safe space where people feel understood, supported, and empowered to transform their lives.
Sakhashree founded Rudraksha Ratna Chakra Therapy (RRCT®️) after years of research into the subtle effects of Rudraksha and gemstones on the human body, mind, and chakras. She created the world's first dedicated Rudraksha website in the year 2000 and has been practising and teaching this sacred science ever since. Her expertise also includes personally sourcing authentic, premium-quality Rudraksha from trusted farms in Nepal and Indonesia, ensuring that every seeker receives genuine and spiritually potent Rudraksha for their healing journey. Rudra Center

A Legacy of Authenticity, Knowledge & Spiritual Wisdom
Founded in 1997, Rudra Centre makes authentic Rudraksha, gemstones and Vedic spiritual knowledge accessible to people around the world. With decades of experience, research and a focus on authenticity, it continues to preserve and share ancient wisdom for modern seekers.
Today, Rudra Centre represents a journey of preserving ancient wisdom while making it accessible, practical and relevant to the modern world.

Chakra Yog

A Path to Inner Awareness, Balance & Transformation
Founded by Sakhashree Neeta in 2012, Chakra Yog brings together Chakra Science and ancient Vedic wisdom to help individuals understand themselves at a deeper level. Through meditation, self-introspection and practical spiritual practices, it encourages greater awareness, balance and inner growth.
At its heart, Chakra Yog is a journey inward—towards greater awareness, inner freedom and the realisation that You Are More.
