Understanding RRCT®

3 Questions 3 Questions

What is Rudraksha Ratna Chakra Therapy (RRCT®)?

RRCT® is a holistic chakra-balancing therapy developed by Sakhashree Neeta. It uses specific combinations of Rudraksha beads and Vedic gemstones, worn as specially designed bracelets and pendants at corresponding chakra locations on the body. RRCT® is designed to identify and balance chakra imbalances associated with emotional blocks, limiting beliefs, stress, and various life challenges.

How is RRCT® different from other healing methods?

RRCT® is a simple and personalised approach that uses Rudraksha and Vedic gemstones to balance the chakras. It is easy to wear, reasonably priced, requires no complicated practices, and creates no ongoing dependency on a therapist.

Is RRCT® a medical treatment?

No. RRCT® is a complementary chakra-balancing therapy, not a medical treatment. Some individuals use it to support their overall well-being while experiencing concerns such as headaches, migraines, sinus discomfort, back or bone pain, digestive discomfort, and circulatory concerns. Individual experiences vary, and RRCT® should not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Chakra Consultation

5 Questions 5 Questions

What happens during a Chakra Consultation?

During your consultation, Sakhashree Neeta will understand your current concerns, assess your chakra energies, identify imbalances, and provide personalised guidance along with suitable RRCT® recommendations to support your journey towards greater balance and well-being.

Who should book a Chakra Consultation?

A consultation may be suitable for anyone experiencing stress or anxiety, emotional imbalance, relationship difficulties, career or business challenges, financial blocks, lack of confidence, low energy or burnout, repeating negative patterns, or a lack of spiritual direction.

Do I need to bring my astrology report?

No. An astrology report or birth chart is not required. RRCT® works with the chakra system, in which each chakra is associated with specific planets and deities. Chakra science takes a deeper and more holistic approach by working with these energies through the chakra system itself. The consultation focuses on identifying chakra imbalances and recommending appropriate Rudraksha and Vedic gemstone combinations rather than relying on horoscope analysis.

Is the consultation online or in person?

Both options are available. You can choose an online or in-person RRCT® consultation based on your preference and availability. Simply select the option most convenient for you when booking.

How long does a consultation last?

Most consultations last between 45 and 90 minutes, depending on your concerns and the level of guidance required.

RRCT® Recommendations

4 Questions 4 Questions

How are the Rudraksha and gemstones selected for me?

Rudraksha and Vedic gemstones are recommended based on your individual chakra assessment and the imbalances identified during your consultation. The combination and placement are personalised according to your specific needs.

What will I receive after my consultation?

You will receive personalised guidance based on your chakra assessment and life chart along with recommendations for suitable Rudraksha and Vedic gemstone combinations and guidance on how and where they should be worn.

Do I have to purchase Rudraksha or gemstones after the consultation?

No. Your consultation provides personalised recommendations and guidance. You can decide whether and when you wish to obtain the recommended Rudraksha or gemstones.

Can I use Rudraksha or gemstones that I already own?

Yes. If you already own Rudraksha beads or gemstones, you can discuss them during your consultation. Their suitability and appropriate placement can be considered as part of your personalised guidance.

Results, Support & Ongoing Practice

4 Questions 4 Questions

What areas of life can RRCT® support?

RRCT® is designed to support chakra balance across different areas of life, including emotional well-being, relationships, confidence, career, finances, personal growth, and spiritual development. Recommendations are personalised according to the chakra imbalances identified during your consultation.

How soon can I expect to notice results from RRCT®?

Every individual’s experience is different. Some people may notice changes sooner, while for others the process may be more gradual. Results can vary depending on the individual, the nature of the imbalance, and consistent use of the recommended RRCT® combination.

Will I need repeated consultations or regular therapy sessions?

RRCT® is designed to be simple and self-supportive once your personalised combination has been recommended. It does not require ongoing dependency on a therapist. You may choose a follow-up consultation if your circumstances change or you feel you need further guidance.

Can I continue meditation, yoga, or other spiritual practices while using RRCT®?

Yes. RRCT® can be used alongside meditation, yoga, mantra chanting, prayer, and other spiritual or wellness practices. It can complement your existing practices as part of your personal journey towards balance and self-awareness.
