The Beginning of Rudra Centre

Rudra Centre began with a simple purpose — to help seekers access authentic Rudraksha along with the knowledge needed to understand, select and use them meaningfully. Over the years, Sakhashree's research and experience expanded this vision to include natural gemstones, Yantras, sacred products, personalised spiritual guidance and Vedic services.

What began with Rudraksha has evolved into a global platform serving seekers around the world.

A Vision Shaped by Research & Experience by Sakashree Neeta

Sakhashree Neeta's journey with Rudra Centre has been closely connected with her study of Rudraksha, gemstones, Chakras and Vedic wisdom.

Through years of research, consultations and interaction with seekers, she developed a deeper understanding of how these ancient tools could be selected and applied with greater awareness.

This journey ultimately contributed to the development of Rudraksha Ratna Chakra Therapy (RRCT®) and later to her broader work in Chakra Science and Chakra Yog.
