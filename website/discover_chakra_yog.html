SEVEN CHAKRAS. SEVEN DIMENSIONS OF LIFE.

Understanding Life through the Chakras

Chakra Yog is based on the understanding that the seven chakras are connected to different dimensions of our lives — from stability, relationships and self-expression to confidence, communication, awareness and spiritual growth.

By understanding our inner patterns, we can become more aware of the beliefs, emotions and behaviours influencing our outer experiences and make more conscious choices.

FROM RESEARCH TO A PATH OF TRANSFORMATION

Sakhashree's Vision Behind Chakra Yog

Sakhashree Neeta's years of research, consultations and study of Rudraksha, gemstones, human behaviour and the chakra system led her to explore a deeper question — how can individuals understand the inner patterns behind the challenges they experience?

Chakra Yog evolved from this journey as a practical path of self-awareness, inner transformation and spiritual growth, bringing together her understanding of Chakra Science with meditation, self-inquiry and experiential learning.

How Chakra Yog is Experienced

Workshops

Experiential programmes that help participants recognise limiting patterns, gain awareness and explore conscious transformation.

Chakra Sadhana

Meditative practices centred around the seven chakras, self-observation and inner awareness.

Personalised Guidance

Individual consultations designed to help people better understand their chakra patterns, strengths and areas of imbalance.

Learning & Certification

Structured programmes that enable individuals to deepen their understanding of Chakra Science and, where applicable, train to guide others.

Taking Chakra Science to the World

Through workshops, consultations, learning programmes and a growing network of trained consultants, Chakra Yog seeks to make authentic Chakra Science accessible to individuals and communities around the world.
