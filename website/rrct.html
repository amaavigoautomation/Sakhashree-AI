Understanding Chakras:

The Root of all Imbalance
Chakras are subtle energy centres located along the spine that regulate human consciousness and biological functioning. The word chakra, meaning “wheel” or “vortex,” describes their nature as dynamic centres through which prana (life force energy) circulates. These centres distribute energy throughout the system via subtle channels known as nadis, similar to how blood circulates through veins in the physical body.

When prana flows freely through the chakras, the body, mind, and emotions remain in harmony. However, prolonged stress, fear, trauma, suppressed emotions, negative conditioning, unhealthy belief systems, lifestyle factors (including irregular routines/indiscipline, poor sleep, lack of physical activity), and unbalanced food habits can cause chakras to become blocked, overactive, or depleted. Over time, this energetic imbalance expresses itself as emotional distress and eventually physical disease.
